Nilphamari

Nilphamari has its own mix of nature, heritage, rivers and local culture. Explore the highlights at a comfortable pace and build your route around the season.

Top Places

Nilsagar

A large historic water body and popular local attraction.

Chini Mosque

A distinctive historic mosque in the district.

Teesta River

Northern river scenery and chars.

Saidpur railway heritage

Historic railway infrastructure and colonial-era urban character.

Suggested Trip Plan

Start with the two nearest highlights in the morning, keep the afternoon for the nature or riverside stop, and leave the final attraction for sunset if access and weather are suitable.
