Rangpur

Rangpur has its own mix of nature, heritage, rivers and local culture. Explore the highlights at a comfortable pace and build your route around the season.

Top Places

Tajhat Palace

A grand historic palace and one of Rangpur's signature attractions.

Carmichael College

A historic educational campus with distinctive architecture.

Keramatiya Mosque

A notable religious and architectural landmark.

Tista River

Northern river landscapes and countryside routes.

Suggested Trip Plan

Start with the two nearest highlights in the morning, keep the afternoon for the nature or riverside stop, and leave the final attraction for sunset if access and weather are suitable.
