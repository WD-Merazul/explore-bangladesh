Cox's Bazar

Bangladesh's famous coastal destination, with a long sandy beach plus nearby hills, islands and fishing communities.

Top Places

Cox's Bazar Sea Beach

The signature attraction for sunrise, sunset and long beach walks.

Himchari

A popular coastal-hill area known for viewpoints, greenery and the waterfall area.

Inani Beach

A quieter scenic beach area with rocky stretches and open sea views.

Maheshkhali

An island destination known for hills, temples, salt fields and local culture.

Suggested Trip Plan

Spend the first half-day around the main beach and Himchari, continue toward Inani, then reserve another day for Maheshkhali if time allows.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
