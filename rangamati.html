Rangamati

A calm hill district centered around Kaptai Lake, boat journeys and green mountain scenery.

Top Places

Kaptai Lake

The district's defining landscape, best experienced from a boat.

Hanging Bridge

A famous Rangamati landmark and a popular short sightseeing stop.

Shuvolong

A scenic lake-and-hill destination commonly visited by boat.

Rajban Vihara

A peaceful Buddhist religious and cultural site in Rangamati town.

Suggested Trip Plan

Keep the morning for the town and Hanging Bridge, then take a boat trip across Kaptai Lake and visit a lake-side destination.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
