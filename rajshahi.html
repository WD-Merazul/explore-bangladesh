Rivers • Heritage • Mango Country

Rajshahi

A relaxed northwestern city known for the Padma riverside, archaeological heritage and seasonal mango culture.

Top Places

Varendra Research Museum

One of the country's important museums for archaeology and regional heritage.

Puthia Temple Complex

A remarkable historic temple landscape in nearby Puthia.

Padma River Bank

A popular place for open skies, sunset and riverside time.

Bagha Mosque

A historic mosque and archaeological landmark in the Rajshahi region.

Suggested Trip Plan

Explore the city and museum first, then visit Puthia and Bagha as a heritage-focused day trip.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
