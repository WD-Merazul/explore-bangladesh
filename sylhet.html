Tea Gardens • Waterfalls • Rivers

Sylhet

A green northeastern destination famous for tea estates, stone rivers, waterfalls and shrine culture.

Top Places

Jaflong

A famous borderland landscape of hills, river and stone collections.

Ratargul Swamp Forest

A freshwater swamp forest best experienced by boat when conditions are suitable.

Lalakhal

Known for scenic river water, boats and surrounding green hills.

Bichanakandi

A rocky river landscape surrounded by hills, especially attractive in the rainy season.

Suggested Trip Plan

Combine Jaflong and Lalakhal in one sightseeing day, then reserve another day for Ratargul and Bichanakandi.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
