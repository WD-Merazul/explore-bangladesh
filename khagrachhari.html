Hills • Caves • Waterfalls

Khagrachhari

A green hill district with winding roads, indigenous culture, waterfalls and forested landscapes.

Top Places

Alutila Cave

A famous natural cave and hill attraction near Khagrachhari town.

Alutila Tourist Center

A convenient viewpoint and stop for enjoying the surrounding hill landscape.

Risang Waterfall

A popular waterfall destination surrounded by green hills.

Sajek route

Khagrachhari is a common gateway for journeys toward Sajek; route conditions should be checked before travel.

Suggested Trip Plan

Explore Alutila and nearby attractions on day one. Use a second day for a longer hill-road journey if road and weather conditions are suitable.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
