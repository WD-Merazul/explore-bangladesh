Hills • Trails • Viewpoints

Bandarban

A mountain destination built for people who enjoy dramatic landscapes, trails, waterfalls and quiet hill roads.

Top Places

Nilgiri

One of the best-known hill viewpoints in Bandarban, especially for cloud and sunrise scenery.

Nafakhum

A remote waterfall destination requiring a more adventurous travel plan.

Boga Lake

A highland lake reached through a demanding hill journey.

Chimbuk

A classic hill viewpoint and road-trip stop with wide mountain scenery.

Suggested Trip Plan

Use one day for nearby viewpoints and another day for a longer trail-based destination. Check local access and weather before remote trips.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
