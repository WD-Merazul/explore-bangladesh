Sea • Rivers • Kuakata

Patuakhali

A southern coastal district best known for Kuakata, wide horizons and riverine landscapes.

Top Places

Kuakata Sea Beach

A famous coastal destination known for broad beach views and sunrise-sunset experiences.

Fatrar Char

A mangrove and coastal nature area reached by boat from the Kuakata side.

Lemur Char

A coastal landscape suited to quiet exploration and photography.

Kuakata Buddhist Temple

A cultural and religious stop near the tourist zone.

Suggested Trip Plan

Base yourself around Kuakata. Use one day for beach and sunrise/sunset, then take a boat-based coastal trip if conditions permit.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
