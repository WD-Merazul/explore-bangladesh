Bagerhat

A historic corner of Bangladesh where medieval architecture, river landscapes and the Sundarbans meet.

Top Places

Sixty Dome Mosque

Historic UNESCO-listed mosque complex and one of the best-known landmarks of Bagerhat.

Khan Jahan Ali Mazar

A major historic religious site associated with Khan Jahan Ali.

Bagerhat Museum

A useful stop for understanding the history and archaeology of the historic mosque city.

Sundarbans Gateway

Bagerhat is an important access area for trips toward the Sundarbans and its waterways.

Suggested Trip Plan

Start with the Sixty Dome Mosque and museum in the morning, visit Khan Jahan Ali Mazar, then keep the afternoon for a relaxed riverside or Sundarbans-oriented plan.

Always check local weather, road/boat access and current site rules before travelling.
